Women in Mathematics Organisations

This page brings together a selection of organisations, networks, and research communities that support women in mathematics, statistics, data science, and related fields. These organisations provide opportunities for networking, mentoring, research collaboration, professional development, and greater visibility for women researchers.

Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM)

A professional association supporting women and girls across the mathematical sciences through research networks, mentoring, conferences, awards, and other professional activities.

Visit Website

European Women in Mathematics (EWM)

A European network connecting women mathematicians and supporting their participation, visibility, collaboration, and professional development in mathematics.

Visit Website

IMU Committee for Women in Mathematics (CWM)

The Committee for Women in Mathematics of the International Mathematical Union promotes international initiatives supporting women mathematicians and connects organisations and networks around the world.

Visit Website

Caucus for Women in Statistics and Data Science

A professional community supporting women in statistics and data science and promoting an inclusive environment for education, research, leadership, and professional development.

Visit Website

Women in Probability

A research community for women working in probability and related areas. The network promotes the visibility of women's research and provides networking and mentoring opportunities, particularly for early-career researchers.

Visit Website

Women in Number Theory (WIN)

An international research network that brings together women working in number theory and encourages research collaboration, professional connections, and greater visibility within the field.

Visit Website
